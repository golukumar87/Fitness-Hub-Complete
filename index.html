    ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

    Everything you need to know about our gym

    What are the gym timings?
    We are open 5:00 AM to 11:00 PM, 7 days a week. We're open 365 days a year!
    What membership plans do you offer?
    We offer three plans: Basic (₹999/month), Premium (₹1999/month), and Gold (₹2999/month). Each plan comes with different benefits.
    Do you have personal trainers?
    Yes! We have 10+ certified personal trainers who can guide you through your fitness journey.
    What facilities do you offer?
    We offer state-of-the-art equipment, locker rooms, shower facilities, yoga studio, cardio area, free weights zone, and dedicated personal training areas.
    Can I cancel my membership?
    Yes, you can cancel your membership with a 30-day notice period. Please contact our front desk for the cancellation process.
    Do you offer student discounts?
    Yes! Students get a 15% discount on all membership plans. Just show your valid student ID at the time of registration.
